Thin lines are the outline structure (what sits inside what); the other lines are cross-links. Double-click a group to fold or unfold it, double-click anything else to focus on its neighbours. Drag a node to place it. Click a line to change or remove a relation. Everything saves to brain/brain.json; the same data works from the terminal with py -3.13 brain/brain.py.
